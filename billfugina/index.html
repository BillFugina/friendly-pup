What I do

Architect

Every system bigger than a script is made of parts, and those parts have to fit. I look for the sweet spot between quick and dirty and over-engineered: clear boundaries, adapters around vendors, and designs a team can keep changing.

Developer

I write code every day and enjoy it. Full stack TypeScript, React, tRPC, Drizzle, Postgres and serverless on Vercel are home turf, and I'm building agent-first products with Claude and MCP.

Lead and mentor

I've led architecture and teams through full product rewrites. Working alongside developers who want to get better is one of the best parts of the job, and I usually learn as much as I teach.
